AI Executive Physics Interpretation

1. Buoyancy Elimination Physics In 1g Earth conditions, hot gases expand, lower in density, and float upward creating a teardrop flame. In ISS 0g microgravity, buoyancy is absent ($Gr \approx 0$). Mass transport is governed purely by radial molecular diffusion ($D \approx 0.2 \text{ cm}^2/\text{s}$).
2. Droplet Micro-Explosion Dynamics Liquid n-Heptane droplets trap dissolved volatile components during combustion. Without buoyant flow to refresh the outer liquid boundary, internal liquid superheating exceeds the nucleation limit ($T_{nuc} \approx 470\text{ K}$), resulting in sudden vapor bubble explosion.
3. CO2 Radiative Quenching Adding Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$) increases atmospheric heat capacity ($C_p$) and radiative emission losses. When radiative heat loss exceeds chemical heat release rate, the microgravity flame quenches safely into full extinction.
